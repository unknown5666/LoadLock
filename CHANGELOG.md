# Changelog

## 11.1.0 (2026-10-31)

### Load charts
- SANY STC1000 (13.5–52 m): 0, 8.52, 14.48 and 19.46 t counterweight, with 10.7 m and 18 m fly jibs at 0, 15 and 30°. Read from the maker's brochure.
- SANY STC1500S added: 0 to 42 t counterweight, full and half outriggers, fly jibs 10.7 to 32 m. Read from SANY's own brochure.
- ZAT4000V: 18 scanned charts added, each read twice.
- Intermediate and reduced-base charts added for ZTC1000V, LTM1500, AC500 and AC700.
- Capacity, hook load and % update live with outriggers, counterweight, boom, jib, radius and slew. Green below 75 %, amber to 90 %, red above.
- A set-up with no chart shows "Chart not loaded" and has no capacity. No interpolation options remain.

### Identity and clients
- Profile page: name, title, certifications, phone, email, signature.
- "Who is this plan for?": client, logo, project, site, reference and contact. Clients are saved for repeat jobs.
- Reports carry the crane operator's branding. The prepared-by block reads "Saad Khan, for City Star Cranes".

### Quick setup
- New plans start with a six-step wizard that picks the crane set-up, the boom and the sections. Switched-off sections leave no trace in the report.

### Drawings
- Scale bars, stated scale, north arrow, exclusion zone and barrier rings, outrigger and hook-height dimensions, power-line clearance check.

### Reports
- New design and order, full-page landscape plan and elevation, utilisation gauge, sign-off page, client colour taken from the logo.
- Nothing in the PDF or its metadata names the software. Author is the signed-in user.
